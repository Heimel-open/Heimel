# Frontier Garden research synthesis — 2026-08-21

Status: informative research synthesis

This note records the source-grounded state used to seed FG-011 through FG-020. It does not promote bounded experiment results into universal claims and does not define runtime authority.

## Combined architecture hypothesis

The current evidence supports a bounded working decomposition:

```text
Frontier Garden = unresolved and latent research possibility
Twin / Framleis = represented state and lineage continuity
Synapse / Mesh = relation, routing and persistent interaction state
Evaluator / consequence = evidence for research-state selection
REHT / lawful authority = separate consequence-bearing authorization
```

The model is replaceable compute. The represented state, semantic bindings, relation history, evidence and authority boundaries are separate objects.

## FG-SYN-R22-R23 — retention by future reach

Synapse R22 was a valid negative result: max-min semantic diversity did not generalize across the frozen held-out distribution and hash-random retention performed better. R23 then reported a valid bounded positive result for target-independent marginal one-step semantic closure gain: 23 exact recoveries across K budgets versus 13 for diversity and a 19.33 hash-random mean. The result is finite deterministic Boolean composition only.

## FG-SYN-RM-FORMATION — regulatory-memory formation

Regulatory traces can bias later regeneration without storing the complete phenotype. Learned incomplete bias transferred to a related unseen phenotype, but an early pre-solution poising rule was not reliable. The open question is therefore how useful latent state forms before exact success, not whether a hand-built or post-success trace can help.

## FG-SYN-RM4-RM5 — retention and forgetting

Conservative latent retention preserved uncertain relations, while safe forgetting succeeded when deletion was restricted to certified semantic duplicates. Low score or current inactivity was not sufficient deletion evidence.

## FG-SYN-R28-R29 — identity formation

R28 did not identify a target-blind natural core. R29 first selected a demonstrated stable five-capability phenotype and then protected the minimum four-relation cover jointly carrying it. The result was 25 of 25 exact continuity outcomes on unseen turnover schedules. This is a bounded computational phenotype, not personal identity.

Sources:

## FG-SYN-R30-R31 — protected expansion

R30 preserved the formed identity in 150 of 150 episodes but acquired only three of six expansion targets. R31 changed only the plastic struggle regime. Persistent-retention acquired all six targets, including all three R30 misses, with about 58 percent fewer evaluations and transport than reset while preserving 360 of 360 identity episodes. Pressure alone did not explain the effect. The next required falsifier is a frozen pristine-family replication.

- https://github.com/nsolland/synapse-lab/pull/57

## FG-SYN-RM6 — semantic bridge

RM-6 transferred an incomplete history-derived developmental disposition across 512 fresh mixed invertible representations only with a supplied correct semantic bridge. Direct coordinate reuse and misbound bridges returned close to the symmetric distribution. This supports bridge-dependent portability, not representation-free memory. The next test must learn enough of the bridge online.

Source:
- https://github.com/nsolland/synapse-lab/pull/60

## FG-SYN-TWIN — derivation and novelty

Twin historical replay implements a candidate surface for testing whether later work was derivable from an earlier represented state. The primary v4 cohort was internally reproducible, but secondary review found summary and schema defects and required rework. The collaborative-frontier protocol defines derivation, Synapse, human-only and joint lanes, but it was not run. Twin remains a representation and lineage mechanism, not the person, truth or authority.

## FG-SYN-SWARM — topology and role diversity

Targeted communication work supports learning who should receive which information. Other current evidence warns that dense communication, hub dominance and premature authority can suppress diversity or collapse higher-order interaction. These are external design signals, not a completed Garden result.

## FG-SYN-AUTHORITY — commit boundary

Garden adjudication, Twin continuity, model routing and learning-evaluator commits are informative or research-state operations. They do not grant execution authority. The durable boundary remains:

```text
research observation -> evaluation -> represented-state commit
                                      !=
lawful admissibility -> execution authorization -> effect -> receipt
```

## Evidence boundary

The Synapse results are controlled repository experiments with workflow artifacts. Many were developed as stacked draft PRs before canonical integration. They are not independent replication and do not establish general intelligence, consciousness, life, biological identity or open-ended semantic learning. Negative results remain part of the evidence and must not be erased by later positive results.
