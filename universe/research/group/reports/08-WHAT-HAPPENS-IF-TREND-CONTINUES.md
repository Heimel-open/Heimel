# What Happens If the Trend Continues

## The Projection Model

**Incident Frequency = Execution Surface × Failure Rate × Visibility Rate**

Current state (2024-2026):
- Execution Surface: Growing 10-20× per year
- Failure Rate: 0.1-1% of actions
- Visibility Rate: 2-20%
- Total incidents: 80-800 over 2 years

Growth assumptions:
- Execution Surface: 10-50× per year (2026-2028), then decelerating
- Failure Rate: constant or increasing if governance falls behind
- Visibility Rate: constant unless mandatory reporting is introduced

## Scenario 1: Conservative (Low Growth, Improving Governance)

**Assumptions:** Execution surface grows 5×/year, failure rate improves to 0.01-0.1%, visibility increases to 20-80% (mandatory reporting).

| Timeframe | Total Incidents | Critical | Economic Impact |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-----------------|
| 12 months | 100-500 | 25-125 | $5-50B |
| 3 years | 500-2,500/yr | 125-625/yr | $50-500B/yr |
| 5 years | 2,500-12,500/yr | 625-3,125/yr | $500B-5T/yr |

**Characteristics:** Governance improves but not fast enough. Multiple catastrophic failures. Tail risk material but manageable with intervention.

## Scenario 2: Expected (Current Trajectory)

**Assumptions:** Execution surface grows 10-20×/year, failure rate constant 0.1-1%, visibility constant 2-20%.

| Timeframe | Total Incidents | Critical | Economic Impact |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-----------------|
| 12 months | 400-4,000 | 100-1,000 | $20-200B |
| 3 years | 4,000-40,000/yr | 1,000-10,000/yr | $200B-2T/yr |
| 5 years | 40,000-400,000/yr | 10,000-100,000/yr | $2-20T/yr |

**Characteristics:** AI failures become systemic risk. Multiple industries affected. Economic impact reaches trillions. Regulatory intervention urgent but still reactive.

## Scenario 3: Accelerated (Rapid Capability, Governance Lag)

**Assumptions:** Execution surface grows 20-50×/year, failure rate increases to 1-10% (governance falls behind), visibility constant.

| Timeframe | Total Incidents | Critical | Economic Impact |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-----------------|

## Scenario 4: Severe Risk (Uncontrolled Expansion, No Governance)

**Assumptions:** Execution surface grows 50-100×/year, failure rate 10-50% (no governance), visibility constant.

| Timeframe | Total Incidents | Critical | Economic Impact |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-----------------|
| 12 months | 5,000-50,000 | 1,250-12,500 | $250B-2.5T |

| 5 years | 500,000-5,000,000/yr | 125,000-1,250,000/yr | $25-250T/yr |

**Characteristics:** AI failures are global existential risk. Multiple industries destroyed. Potential systemic economic collapse.
