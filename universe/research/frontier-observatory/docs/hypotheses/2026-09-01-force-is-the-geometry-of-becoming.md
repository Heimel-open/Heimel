# Force is the geometry of becoming

Date: 2026-09-01  
Status: FROZEN — canonical research hypothesis  
Origin: Njål Gaute Solland  
Evidence status: necessary-component hypothesis; geometry and transition law remain UNKNOWN

## Canonical formulation

> **Force is the geometry of becoming.**

## Epistemic boundary

We do not yet know:

- which geometry describes becoming;
- what the relevant future space is;
- which paths count as distinct;
- which futures count as viable;
- which forms of force or adversity are relevant;
- whether one invariant works across systems;
- whether closure is sharp, distributed, or scale-dependent.

The current claim is narrower: causal constraint, a structured space of possible continuations, paths through that space, and a change in causal organization appear to be necessary components of the transition we seek. This is a research hypothesis, not a discovered law.

## Working meaning

Force is used in a general causal sense, not only as Newtonian force.

The candidate idea is that consequential force changes the structure of available continuations. It may open, close, narrow, weight, merge, or make paths unreachable. Becoming is the realized continuation produced through that changing structure.

Candidate formulation:

> **Force is the causal transformation of the geometry of possible futures. Becoming is the realized continuation through that transformation.**

The words *geometry*, *future*, *path*, *viability*, and *force* remain operational variables to be discovered rather than assumed.

## The TADA moment

The TADA moment is not the first observation of a new property. That is detection and may occur after the causal transition.

For a chosen system, intervention set, viability criterion, and resolution, define the candidate TADA moment (t^*) as the earliest transition where all four conditions hold:

1. **Before:** immediately before (t^*), at least one viable continuation exists without the candidate new organization.
2. **After:** immediately after (t^*), no viable continuation exists without that organization.
3. **Necessity:** removing the transition-specific organization removes the new capacity or continued viability.
4. **Restoration:** restoring that organization restores the capacity or continued viability.

Canonical statement:

> **The point of becoming is the earliest causally verified transition where the last viable continuation outside the new organization disappears. There.**

## Measurement protocol

For every developmental transition (t_i ightarrow t_{i+1}):

1. Snapshot the complete observable system and environment on both sides.
2. Enumerate or sample future continuations under a frozen intervention policy.
3. Classify viability using criteria fixed before inspecting the transition outcome.
4. Identify which continuations remain possible without the candidate organization.
5. Ablate only the transition-specific change.
6. Replay matched controls from the same pre-transition state.
7. Restore the change and replay again.
8. Repeat across seeds, histories, environments, and resolutions.
9. Record the earliest boundary satisfying Before, After, Necessity, and Restoration.
10. Report uncertainty when the boundary can only be localized to an interval.

The measured result must be one of:

- **TADA-POINT:** one transition is resolved and passes all four conditions;
- **TADA-INTERVAL:** the transition lies within a bounded interval but cannot yet be resolved further;
- **DISTRIBUTED-BECOMING:** no single transition passes, but a necessary transition set does;
- **NO-CLOSURE:** the capacity appears without demonstrated closure of alternatives;
- **UNRESOLVED:** instrumentation or future-space coverage is insufficient.

## Required controls

A claimed TADA moment is invalid without:

- matched pre/post snapshots;
- an unchanged-history control;
- ablation of the candidate organization;
- restoration or reconstruction;
- alternative changes with comparable size but different structure;
- blinded viability classification where possible;
- explicit future-space coverage;
- resolution-sensitivity analysis;
- independent replay.

Correlation, first detection, performance increase, phase-transition appearance, or network density alone cannot establish TADA.

## Adversity hypothesis

Adversity matters only when it has causal consequence. Noise or difficulty that leaves the viable continuation set unchanged is not sufficient.

Adversity can also destroy. Therefore the claim is not that adversity always creates development.

The testable candidate is:

> **When continued viability remains possible but the existing organization is no longer causally sufficient, consequential constraint forces reorganization.**

Or:

> **I am forced to become.**

## Intelligence implication

Intelligence, or the potential for intelligence, is causal to the extent that an organization changes which future states can be reached, avoided, reconstructed, or selected.

Capability is not established by appearance, description, or measured novelty alone. It is established when the organization makes a reproducible counterfactual difference to future realization.

## Novelty boundary

The components have intellectual predecessors in process philosophy, theories of becoming and force, general relativity, dynamical systems, viability theory, causal emergence, phase transitions, and constraint-based accounts of organization.

The frozen contribution is the candidate synthesis:

> **force → transformation of future geometry → closure of alternatives → necessary becoming**

No claim of scientific priority or universal law is made without full literature review, formalization, and experimental survival.

## Falsification

The hypothesis fails or must be revised if:

1. the proposed closure boundary cannot be identified independently of the later property;
2. matched closure occurs without the predicted reorganization;
3. reorganization reliably occurs while the prior viable continuation class remains causally available;
4. ablation leaves capacity and viability unchanged;
5. restoration fails to restore them;
6. a simpler variable predicts the transition and closure adds no explanatory or predictive value;
7. the result disappears under reasonable changes in resolution, viability definition, or intervention coverage.

## Research target

Discover the minimum exact combination of:

- causal constraint;
- relational geometry;
- viable-path closure;
- retained global organization;

at which emergence will occur, not merely can occur.
