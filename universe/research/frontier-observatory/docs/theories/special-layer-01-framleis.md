## Framleis

**Framleis shall not be translated.**

Framleis is established here as a linguistic primitive in the Phi-Law.

Working understanding:

> Framleis is the continuity that survives transformation while carrying the history of what has been lost.

Framleis is more than:

- still
- yet
- continuing
- remaining

Framleis carries:

- time
- loss
- transformation
- continuity
- the presence of history

Important distinction:

```text
Still is a state.

Framleis is a history.
```

Framleis says:

```text
Eg er ikkje den same.

Eg er heller ikkje ein annan.

Eg er gjennom alt dette.

Eg er her framleis.
```

---

## Linguistic Primitive

A linguistic primitive is not primitive in the everyday sense.

It is a ground-word: a term that should not be reduced to other terms too early.

Examples from other traditions include:

- Tao
- Logos
- Dasein
- Sunyata
- Ubuntu
- Wabi-sabi

Framleis is used in the same spirit.

It is not left untranslated for exotic effect.

It is left untranslated because direct translation loses the very continuity the term carries.

## Identity

Identity is not treated here as a static object or substance.

Working hypothesis:

> Identity is the trace left by transformation that makes further transformation possible.

This avoids the circularity of defining a coherent system as a system that preserves identity, while defining identity as what a coherent system preserves.

---

## Selection

The session clarified that loss is not merely deletion.

A transforming system must recognize:

```text
This we let go.

This we carry forward.
```

Selection is therefore not merely filtering.

Selection is recognition.

---

## Coherence

The current definition of coherence is not yet final.

The circular problem remains:

```text
A coherent system preserves identity.

Identity is what a coherent system preserves.
```

The proposed escape route is to start from transformation rather than from system or identity.

Working direction:

```text
What makes the next transformation possible?
```

Possible working definition:

> A coherent system is a system that repeatedly makes the next transformation possible.

This definition is recursive rather than statically circular.
