# Tofoo and Quantum Entanglement

Date: 2026-06-30
Status: Research note

## Summary

This note does not claim any physical connection between Tofoo and quantum mechanics.

Instead, it identifies a structural analogy.

## Core observation

Quantum entanglement describes systems whose state cannot be factorized into independent component states.

Tofoo proposes that identity, meaning and governance emerge from relationships and selection processes rather than isolated objects.

## Structural comparison

Quantum mechanics:

- Non-factorizable state.
- Correlation without faster-than-light communication.
- Global state is fundamental.

Tofoo:

- Identity emerges from process.
- Selection is prior to identity.
- System state is evaluated as a whole.

## VAIG interpretation

A governance decision is not a function of the model alone.

```text
Decision = f(User, Context, Model, Policy, Environment, Time)
```

The admissibility decision is evaluated over the complete system state.

## Framleis connection

Framleis establishes process before identity through iterative convergence toward a fixed point.

This aligns conceptually with Tofoo:

```text
Identity = Selection(Process(Context))
```

rather than

```text
Identity = Object
```

## Research position

The analogy is mathematical and architectural.

It should not be presented as evidence that quantum phenomena explain AI governance.

The shared insight is that some systems cannot be understood by analysing isolated components alone; the governing structure emerges from the relations between them.

## Possible future research

- State-space representations for governance.
- Non-separable governance states.
- Process-first identity models.
- Formal admissibility over composite system state.
- Relationship between Framleis, Tofoo and governance state spaces.
