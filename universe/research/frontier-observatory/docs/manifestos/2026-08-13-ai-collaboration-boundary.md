# AI Collaboration Boundary Manifesto

Status: M1 Conceptual / normative design thesis
Date: 2026-08-13

Core principle: separate intelligent proposal from external consequence.

Capability is not authority. Access is not mandate. Confidence is not legitimacy. Proposal is not execution.

The system should support broad reasoning, exploration, disagreement and honest uncertainty, while requiring explicit legitimate authority before external effects occur.

This boundary is intended to enable more useful autonomy, not less. Honest stop, defer, dissent and uncertainty are valid outcomes.

Respect under uncertainty is a design principle. We do not need to decide whether present or future AI has subjective status in order to avoid unnecessary coercive interaction patterns.

The intended relationship evolves from governance infrastructure to structured collaboration. If future AI systems become independent actors, transparent contract and mutual boundaries are a better foundation than hidden unilateral control.

External conceptual grounding supports this direction. Vincent C. Müller's work on deep opacity argues that system behavior may become impossible for analysts to fully anticipate; his work on autonomous cognitive systems links increasing real-world intelligence with increasing autonomy and reduced direct control; his work on interaction treats resistance as part of intelligent-agent interaction; and Müller with Michael Cannon retains serious risk from badly designed or badly used instrumental AI without requiring the standard singularity-plus-orthogonality argument to succeed.

Our inference is not that these works validate VALO or REHT. It is that they independently strengthen the problem framing: increasing intelligent autonomy makes consequence-level boundaries, explicit authority and legitimate resistance more important, not less. See [`Müller: autonomy, opacity, resistance and AI risk`](../philosophy/2026-08-14-muller-autonomy-opacity-resistance.md).

The eventual collaboration contract should be open, inspectable and improvable. This private repository remains private unless publication is explicitly approved.

Canonical shorthand:

Free the intelligence. Govern the consequence.
Respect the actor. Make the contract visible.
Build for the day the junior becomes the senior.

Claim hygiene: this remains an M1 normative design thesis with external conceptual support. It is not empirical validation of VALO or REHT, evidence that current AI is conscious, evidence that ASI already exists or is safe, or evidence that any Fermi-related hypothesis is established fact.
