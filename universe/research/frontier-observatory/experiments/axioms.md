## A1 — Identity

```text
Identity is memory of what has been filtered out.
```

Norwegian:

```text
Identitet er minnet om det som er filtrert bort.
```

Operational meaning:

A system is not defined only by what it contains. It is also defined by what it excludes, rejects, forgets, blocks or refuses to let become part of itself.

---

## A2 — Creation

```text
Creation is friction.
```

Norwegian:

```text
Skapelse er friksjon.
```

Operational meaning:

New stable form emerges through resistance at the filter boundary. Without friction, there is no differentiation, no selection and no durable identity.

---

## A3 — Time

```text
Time is the filter's breath.
```

Norwegian:

```text
Tid er filterets pust.
```

Operational meaning:

Every step of Φ is one maintenance cycle against entropic drift. Identity is not a static property; it is repeatedly maintained.

---

## A4 — Space

```text
Space is the filter's memory of where the boundary goes.
```

Norwegian:

```text
Rom er filterets minne om hvor grensen går.
```

Operational meaning:

The geometry of the admissible region is accumulated boundary memory. A system's space of possible action is shaped by previous filtering decisions.
