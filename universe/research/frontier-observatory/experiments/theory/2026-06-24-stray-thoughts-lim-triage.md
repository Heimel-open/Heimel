## 1. Fractional nonlinear Schrodinger equation

Signal:

```text
wave evolution + nonlocal dispersion + nonlinear self-interaction
```

LIM relevance:

Medium.

Useful because it combines three ideas close to LIM:

```text
evolution over time
nonlocal memory / dispersion
self-feedback
```

Possible LIM mapping:

```text
wave state = evolving identity
fractional kinetic term = nonlocal memory / long-range dependency
nonlinear term = self-interaction / feedback
stability = maintained form under perturbation
```

Maturity:

M1/M2 only.

Do not claim direct relation unless a real mapping between fractional dynamics and tau is derived.

Verdict:

Keep as mathematical metaphor.
Do not promote as evidence yet.
