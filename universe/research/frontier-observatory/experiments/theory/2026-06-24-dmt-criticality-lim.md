# DMT, criticality and LIM

Date: 2026-06-24
Status: theory note
Maturity: M2/M4 split

## Claim

The DMT criticality findings are relevant to LIM because they support a narrow version of the core identity-maintenance claim:

```text
A stable sense of self appears to require active maintenance near a critical operating regime.
```

This maps cleanly to LIM:

```text
Identity is not a static substance.
Identity is maintained by a filtering process over time.
```

## Source anchor

Primary paper:

```text
Irrmischer, Aqil, Luan, Wang, Engelbregt, Carhart-Harris, Linkenkaer-Hansen, Timmermann.
DMT-Induced Shifts in Criticality Correlate with Self-Dissolution.
Journal of Neuroscience, 2025.
DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0344-25.2025
PMID: 41285580
```

Key finding:

DMT shifts brain oscillations away from criticality in alpha and adjacent frequency bands. Entropy increases while complexity decreases. The criticality shifts in alpha and theta bands correlate with self-dissolution. The shift is toward subcritical regimes.

## LIM mapping

### A1 — Identity is filtered memory

The self depends on continuity: past narrative, present interpretation and future prediction.

DMT weakens the time-extended self. This supports A1 in a limited neuroscientific sense:

```text
Self = maintained narrative boundary
Ego dissolution = temporary boundary weakening
```

### A3 — Time is filter recurrence

The reported shift toward present-only experience is directly relevant to A3.

Normal selfhood appears to require recurrence across time: memory, prediction and updating.

DMT disrupts that recurrence.

```text
If recurrence weakens, the self becomes less temporally extended.
```

This is close to the LIM claim that identity is not a fixed object but a repeated maintenance operation.

### Criticality as Goldilocks analogue

The study supports a general Goldilocks structure:

```text
Too ordered -> rigid / low flexibility
Near criticality -> coherent adaptive self
Too disorganized or subcritical -> self-dissolution / weakened narrative continuity
```

Important: this is a structural analogue, not proof of the exact LIM constants.

The paper does not validate:

## Strongly supported

1. Conscious selfhood depends on ongoing neural dynamics, not a static inner object.
2. DMT disrupts alpha/theta criticality signatures.
3. The disruption correlates with self-dissolution.
4. Brain criticality is a plausible operating regime for coherent self-maintenance.

## Moderately supported LIM interpretation

DMT can be interpreted as a temporary perturbation of the neural identity-maintenance filter.

Under this interpretation:

```text
alpha rhythms = candidate carrier of time-extended self-maintenance
criticality = candidate neural Goldilocks regime
self-dissolution = temporary loss of narrative boundary coherence
```

## Weak / not yet supported

1. Alpha waves are not proven to be "the carrier frequency of the ego."
2. The claustrum claim requires separate source verification and should not be included as canonical LIM evidence yet.
3. The study does not show that normal consciousness is merely hallucination; that is a broader theoretical interpretation.
4. The study does not calibrate LIM tau.
5. The study does not prove Phi-law as a universal natural law.
