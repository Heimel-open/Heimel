Definition

H — hidden state matrix (seq_len × hidden_dim)
SVD: H = U Σ VT
pi = σi² / Σ σj²    (normalised squared singular values)
Hsp = −Σ pi ln pi    (spectral entropy)
reff = exp(Hsp)    (effective rank)
τ = reff / min(seq_len, hidden_dim)

Goldilocks bounds derived analytically — not from any benchmark dataset:

τmin = exp(−γ) ≈ 0.5615    (Euler-Mascheroni constant)
τmax = 1/ζ(3) ≈ 0.8319    (Apéry's constant)

The Structural Claim

No current model is inside the Goldilocks interval. Not because the law is wrong — because models are not yet large enough to reach geometric coherence. τ rises with scale. The ordering coherent > random > repetitive is stable across all models and all layers. The law has not been falsified. It has issued a diagnosis and a prediction.

The bounds [exp(−γ), 1/ζ(3)] cannot be benchmark-optimised. They are structural — derived from the spectral geometry of the hidden state space. A model either has sufficient internal dimensionality or it does not.
