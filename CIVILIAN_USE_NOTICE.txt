HEIMEL OPEN — CIVILIAN TECHNOLOGY AND USE NOTICE

Heimel is developed and published as civilian, general-purpose software infrastructure for governing the transition from intent to consequence.

Heimel is not designed or supplied as:
- a weapon or weapon component;
- military command-and-control software;
- targeting, fire-control, weapons-delivery or combat-management software;
- intelligence collection or espionage software; or
- a surveillance system.

The Heimel core is domain-agnostic. Its purpose is to enforce current authority, exact-action boundaries, exclusive governed effect paths, fail-closed behavior and verifiable evidence across civilian software and autonomous systems.

DEFENCE AND MILITARY USE

Defence, military, intelligence, targeting, weapons, combat and offensive-security use are not intended or supported use cases of Heimel Open.
