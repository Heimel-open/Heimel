# Boundary-constrained coherence

**Status:** research hypothesis · **maturity:** formalized proposal/simulation

## Proposition

Coherence in a changing system may depend on both internal coupling and a
meaning-preserving boundary. A boundary alone is insufficient: stochastic drift,
noise and adversarial inputs must also be measured.

## Relation to Heimel

This is an interpretive research lens for continuity and integrity. It does not
authorize action, define operative state or replace the Heimel consequence
boundary.

## Falsifiers

Reject or narrow the claim if boundary-constrained systems do not outperform
unconstrained baselines under held-out perturbations, or if apparent coherence
disappears when phase noise and representation changes are controlled.
