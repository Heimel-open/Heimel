# LIM / identity maintenance

**Status:** research hypothesis · **maturity:** conceptual/simulation-backed

## Proposition

An open system that maintains identity over time needs a selective filter: it
must preserve some state while rejecting noise, contradiction or drift. The
filter is a candidate explanation for continuity, not a universal law.

## Evidence boundary

Current support consists of project simulations and theoretical synthesis. It
does not establish universal constants, a universal equation or a validated
physical law. Independent replication and domain-specific measurement remain
required.

## Falsifiers

The hypothesis should be narrowed or rejected if comparable systems maintain
identity without selective retention, or if the proposed filter does not
improve continuity against a measured baseline.
