# Representation continuity

**Status:** research hypothesis · **maturity:** evaluation proposal

## Proposition

An internal representation is not the thing it represents. A system should be
tested across validated meaning-preserving representations rather than judged
from one wording, layout or encoding. Stable meaning with materially different
decisions is representation drift.

## Measurement

Compare the same task and outcome across semantically equivalent representations
and report aggregate, worst-case and best-case results separately. Record which
transformations were validated as meaning-preserving.

## Boundary

This is evaluation evidence, not authority. It cannot approve an action, mint
operative state or replace fresh consequence-time authorization.
