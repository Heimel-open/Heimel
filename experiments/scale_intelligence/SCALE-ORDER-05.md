# SCALE-ORDER-05 — Integration–differentiation transition test

Status before execution: PREREGISTERED.

## Question

Does independently measured task capability peak at an interior interaction scale where the fixed substrate is sufficiently integrated to coordinate globally but still preserves meaningful local distinctions?

This operationalizes the candidate idea "between confusion and understanding" as a bounded toy-substrate hypothesis. It does **not** define human understanding, consciousness, or general intelligence.

## Prior evidence boundary

SCALE-SEPARATION-02 found that task success, transfer and distributed integration were scale-sensitive and peaked around an interior scale on this substrate. That result is hypothesis-generating here; it is not counted as confirmation of SCALE-ORDER-05.

Fresh seeds are required.

## Frozen substrate

Reuse `scale_sweep.py` unchanged:

- 63 nodes on the same distance-labelled ring;
- scales `1, 2, 4, 8, 16`;
- 3 synchronous rounds;
- 4 peers per node per round;
- one scalar state channel;
- same update rule;
- same segmented, transfer and counterfactual task generators;
- 256 episodes per family;
- same invariant digests for node set, topology, initial-state encoding, taskset, compute budget and update rule.

Fresh replicate seeds: `7007, 8008, 9009, 10010, 11011`.

Only `interaction_scale` varies within a replicate.

## Frozen measurements

### Integration

Use the existing `distributed_integration` score from `scale_sweep.run_replicate()`:

`1 - mean absolute error(final node state, true global mean)`, clipped to `[0,1]`.

### Differentiation

On the exact segmented episodes already generated by the frozen runner, remove the global mean from both input and final node states. Compute:

1. positive centered correlation between initial and final local deviations;
2. retained centered amplitude `min(1, std(final_centered) / std(initial_centered))`;
3. differentiation = their product, clipped to `[0,1]`.

Thus unrelated variance does not count as differentiation, and complete homogenization scores zero.

The segmented stream is recreated offline from the same seed solely to instrument this metric. It does not feed information back into the substrate.

### Capability

Capability is independent of both integration and differentiation:

`0.5 * (task_success + cross_context_transfer)`.

The integration metric is deliberately excluded from capability to avoid making the target tautological.

### Balance

`harmonic_mean(integration, differentiation)`.

Balance is descriptive. The primary empirical target remains independent task capability.

## Preregistered gates

Let the candidate peak be the best capability scale among the three interior scales `2, 4, 8`.

SCALE-ORDER-05 survives only if all gates hold:

1. candidate capability exceeds scale 1 by at least `0.05`;
2. candidate capability exceeds scale 16 by at least `0.05`;
3. candidate integration exceeds scale 1 integration by at least `0.05`;
4. candidate differentiation exceeds scale 16 differentiation by at least `0.05`;
5. candidate balance is within `0.02` of the maximum balance observed across all five scales;
6. candidate capability exceeds both extreme scales in at least `4/5` paired fresh replicates.

Five valid replicates are required at every scale. Any invariant drift yields `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

No threshold may be changed after the run.

## Interpretation

`NOT_FALSIFIED_BY_DATA` would support only this bounded claim:

> On this fixed toy substrate, useful capability is highest in an interior operating regime that combines greater global integration than the low-scale extreme with greater preservation of local distinctions than the high-scale extreme.

It would not establish a universal law, prove an optimal scale for intelligence, or show that the measured transition is equivalent to understanding.

`FALSIFIED_BY_DATA` means the proposed transition-zone account fails under the preregistered metrics/gates on the tested substrate/range.

## Next evidence rule

Record the full negative or surviving result without retuning. Any alternative differentiation metric or altered gate becomes a new protocol.
