# Stratum Industries — GovTech agentic case processing

Source: https://stratumindustries.co/
Captured: 2026-08-09

## Classification

GovTech / agentic case processing / external market validation / potential integration target.

## Signal

Stratum is building large-scale AI-agent processing for public-sector application and case backlogs. Their public positioning centers on parallel agent review, evidence gathering, policy/rule application, auditability, and retaining a human decision-maker.

This is a strong market signal for VALO Public Pack: agentic public-sector case processing is becoming a commercial category rather than a research-only pattern.

## VALO relevance

Stratum's public architecture appears to cover automated case analysis and recommendation. What is not publicly documented is a distinct commit-time execution-governance boundary that proves, for each concrete action:

- current authority and mandate;
- valid delegation and scope;
- admissible and sufficiently fresh evidence;
- causal continuity between assessed state and execution state;
- the exact action authorized;
- post-execution evidence that the authorized action is what actually occurred.

This maps directly to the VALO chain around Authority State -> VAIG -> reht -> deterministic decision contract -> external enforcement -> Veritas.

## Strategic interpretation

Treat Stratum as both external validation and a potential integration/partner target, not simply as a competitor.

The key differentiation is the transition from "AI reviews the case, human makes the decision" to governed execution. As public-sector agents progress from recommendation into mutations such as updating registers, issuing decisions, triggering payments, reserving resources, or changing rights, an explicit execution-authorization boundary becomes materially necessary.

Canonical question:

> When hundreds of agents operate on public-sector cases in parallel, how do we prove that every concrete action was legitimate at the exact moment it executed?
