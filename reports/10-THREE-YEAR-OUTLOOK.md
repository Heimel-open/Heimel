# Three-Year Outlook

## What Will Happen by 2029

The three-year outlook (2027-2029) represents a critical inflection point. By 2029, the execution surface will have expanded 100-1,000× from 2026 levels. The total incident population will reach 500-40,000 incidents per year. The economic impact will reach $50 billion to $2 trillion per year.

This is not a projection based on worst-case assumptions. It is a projection based on observed growth rates, observed failure patterns, and the assumption that governance does not catch up.

## The Execution Surface in 2029

By 2029, AI systems will have:

**1. Broader tool access**
- AI agents will have access to 500-1,000 integrated tools (up from 50-100 in 2026)
- Tools will include financial systems, HR systems, legal systems, infrastructure management, customer service platforms
- The attack surface for failure will expand proportionally

**2. Deeper permissions**
- AI agents will have write access to production systems across multiple domains
- AI agents will have administrative access to infrastructure
- AI agents will have authority over financial transactions worth millions
- The potential impact of each failure will increase proportionally

**3. Higher autonomy**
- AI agents will operate continuously for weeks or months (up from days in 2026)
- AI agents will make decisions without human oversight
- AI agents will coordinate with other AI agents in multi-agent workflows
- The probability of unchecked failure will increase proportionally

**4. Greater financial authority**
- AI agents will authorize transactions worth tens of millions of dollars
- AI agents will manage budgets and allocate resources
- AI agents will execute contracts and payments
- The economic impact of each failure will reach hundreds of millions

**5. Physical-world access**
- AI agents will control autonomous vehicles at scale
- AI agents will control industrial robots and manufacturing systems
- AI agents will control critical infrastructure (power grids, water systems, transportation)
- The safety risk of each failure will increase proportionally

**6. Multi-agent coordination**
- AI agents will coordinate with other AI agents in complex workflows
- Multi-agent orchestration platforms will be deployed at scale
- Emergent behaviors will arise from agent interactions
- New failure modes will emerge that are not predictable from individual agents

## The Incident Landscape in 2029

By 2029, the incident landscape will be characterized by:

**1. High frequency**
- 500-40,000 incidents per year under the expected scenario
- Multiple incidents per week under the conservative scenario
- Multiple incidents per day under the accelerated scenario

**2. High severity**
- 125-10,000 critical incidents per year under the expected scenario
- Multiple catastrophic failures per year
- Tail risk is material and increasing

**3. Systemic scope**
- Incidents will affect multiple industries
- Incidents will affect multiple organizations
- Incidents will cascade across interconnected systems
- The scope of each incident will be larger than incidents in 2026

**4. Economic impact**
- Total economic impact: $50-500 billion per year under the conservative scenario
- Total economic impact: $200B-2T per year under the expected scenario
- Total economic impact: $500B-5T per year under the accelerated scenario
- Cumulative economic impact over 3 years: $1-15 trillion

**5. Novel failure modes**
- New failure modes will emerge that are not predictable from 2026 incidents
- Multi-agent coordination failures will create emergent risks
- Physical-world failures will create safety-critical risks
- Financial failures will create systemic economic risks

## The Key Trends

The key trends driving the three-year outlook:

### Trend 1: Execution Surface Expansion

The execution surface will continue to expand exponentially. By 2029:
- Tool breadth: 10-20× increase from 2026
- Permission depth: 5-10× increase from 2026
- Autonomy level: 3-5× increase from 2026
- Operation duration: 10-20× increase from 2026
- Financial authority: 50-100× increase from 2026
- Physical-world access: 5-10× increase from 2026
- Multi-agent coordination: 10-50× increase from 2026

The compounding effect means that overall risk will increase 10^7 to 10^35 times from 2026 to 2029.

### Trend 2: Governance Lag

Governance will not keep pace with execution surface expansion:
- Regulatory frameworks will be 2-5 years behind capability
- Internal controls will be 1-3 years behind capability
- Industry standards will be 3-5 years behind capability
- Best practices will be 2-4 years behind capability

The governance lag means that AI systems will be deployed with governance frameworks that are significantly behind the current state of capability.

### Trend 3: Incident Frequency and Severity

Incident frequency and severity will increase proportionally with execution surface:
- Incident frequency will increase 10-100× from 2026 to 2029
- Incident severity will increase 5-50× from 2026 to 2029
- Economic impact will increase 10-100× from 2026 to 2029

The increase in frequency and severity means that AI failures will become a major economic and operational risk.

### Trend 4: Tail Risk

Tail risk will become material:
- The probability of catastrophic failure will increase significantly
- The impact of catastrophic failure will reach billions or trillions of dollars
- Multiple organizations will experience catastrophic failures
- The cumulative impact of tail risk will be significant

Tail risk will require active management and governance intervention.

### Trend 5: Regulatory Response

Regulatory response will intensify:
- Mandatory reporting requirements may be introduced
- Circuit breaker requirements may be introduced
- Approval gate requirements may be introduced
- Governance standards may be introduced

However, regulatory response will be reactive, not anticipatory. Regulatory frameworks will be developed after incidents occur, not before systems are deployed.

## The Sector-Specific Outlook

The three-year outlook varies by sector:

### Software Development

**Incident frequency:** High
- Production database deletions will become common
- Credential cascades will occur frequently
- Multi-agent coordination failures will emerge

**Severity:** High
- Economic impact: $10-100 billion per year
- Operational impact: Significant disruption to development workflows

**Key risks:**
- Production system destruction
- Credential compromise
- Cascade failures across development tools

### Financial Services

**Incident frequency:** High
- Large-scale financial errors will occur frequently
- Trading algorithm failures will occur
- Customer service AI errors will affect millions

**Severity:** Critical
- Economic impact: $100 billion - $1 trillion per year
- Systemic risk: Financial market disruption

**Key risks:**
- Catastrophic financial errors
- Market manipulation by AI agents
- Regulatory violations and penalties

### Transportation

**Incident frequency:** Moderate-High
- Autonomous vehicle accidents will increase
- Traffic management AI errors will occur
- Logistics coordination failures will happen

**Severity:** Critical
- Safety impact: Multiple fatalities per year
- Economic impact: $50-500 billion per year

**Key risks:**
- Safety-critical failures
- Infrastructure disruption
- Supply chain disruption

### Healthcare

**Incident frequency:** Moderate

- Patient data breaches will happen

**Severity:** Critical
- Safety impact: Multiple fatalities per year
- Economic impact: $20-200 billion per year

**Key risks:**
- Patient safety incidents

### Customer Service

- Mass user action errors will occur

**Severity:** High
- Economic impact: $10-100 billion per year
- Reputational impact: Significant brand damage

**Key risks:**

- Discrimination lawsuits
- Reputational damage

### Critical Infrastructure

**Incident frequency:** Moderate
- Power grid AI errors will occur
- Water system AI errors will happen
- Transportation system AI errors will occur

**Severity:** Critical
- Safety impact: Potential mass casualties
- Economic impact: $100 billion - $1 trillion per year

**Key risks:**
- Infrastructure failure
- Public safety incidents
- National security threats

## The Bottom Line

By 2029, AI failures will be a major economic and operational risk. The frequency of incidents will reach 500-40,000 per year. The economic impact will reach $50-500 billion per year. The tail risk will be material.

The question is not whether this will happen. The question is whether governance will catch up before the failures become catastrophic.
