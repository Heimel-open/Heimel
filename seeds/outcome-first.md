# Seed: Outcome-first measurement

**Seed version:** `outcome-first/0.1`  
**Status:** measurement principle under test  
**Full report:** [Measuring AI by Completed Outcomes](../reports/08-measuring-ai-by-completed-outcomes.md)

## Proposition

AI systems should be compared primarily by legitimate, evidenced outcomes,
not by output volume, token count, activity or apparent autonomy.

## Minimum structure

An outcome-first observation identifies the intended completion, acceptance
criteria, consequence class, responsible evaluation, evidence and unresolved
uncertainty. It records non-completion and blocked work rather than counting
them as partial success without an explicit rule.

## Adoption test

Compare a workflow using activity metrics with one using outcome contracts. The
outcome-first representation should reveal false productivity, hidden human
work, unsafe shortcuts and evidence gaps that activity metrics miss.

## Falsifiers and boundaries

Narrow the seed if outcome contracts cannot improve decisions across materially
different workflows or if they hide meaningful exploratory work. The seed does
not imply that every valuable activity has an immediate measurable outcome, and
it does not authorize execution.
