# Seed: ACE — Attentive Cognitive Evaluation

**Seed version:** `ace/0.1`  
**Status:** research hypothesis  
**Full report:** [The ACE Economy](../reports/01-the-ace-economy.md)

## Proposition

When machine-generated capability becomes abundant, a scarce unit of value is
often the accountable human attention needed to provide context, judgment,
mandate and responsibility for a consequential outcome.

ACE is a lens for measuring that scarce contribution. It is not a claim that
all human work reduces to one universal unit.

## Minimum vocabulary

An ACE observation may record the relevant combination of:

`attention + context + judgment + mandate + accountability`

The observation should identify the decision or outcome being evaluated, the
human contribution, the evidence used, and the uncertainty or disagreement
that remains.

## Adoption test

An adopter should be able to compare two workflows and show whether ACE adds
explanatory power beyond elapsed time, headcount, token volume or revenue. The
measurement must preserve disagreement and responsibility rather than silently
turning them into a productivity score.

## Falsifiers and boundaries

Narrow or reject the seed if it cannot improve measurement across materially
different workflows, or if its scores erase accountability and uncertainty.
ACE does not authorize actions, establish legal responsibility, replace human
judgment or create an execution permission.
