# Seed: GCU — Governed Completion Unit

**Seed version:** `gcu/0.1`  
**Status:** experimental economic/operational hypothesis  
**Full report:** [From Jobs to Governed Completion Units](../reports/02-from-jobs-to-governed-completion-units.md)

## Proposition

A completed, bounded and evidenced piece of consequential work may become a
portable economic unit when the carrier performing it can be substituted
without changing the meaning, acceptance criteria or governance of the work.

GCU is a hypothesis about a measurable work unit, not an established market
standard.

## Minimum structure

A candidate GCU should identify:

`scope + inputs + constraints + carrier + completion criteria + evidence + outcome`

The candidate must distinguish a proposal from a decision, completion from
mere activity, and outcome evidence from authorization.

## Adoption test

Hold the GCU contract and acceptance criteria constant while replacing the
execution carrier. Compare completion quality, evidence integrity, latency,
cost, exceptions and human intervention. The unit is only useful if the
comparison remains meaningful across carriers and domains.

## Falsifiers and boundaries

Narrow or reject the seed if the contract collapses when the carrier changes,
if completion cannot be independently evidenced, or if the unit is only a
renamed task, transaction or labor-hour metric. GCU does not authorize an
effect, settle a payment, establish operative state or replace REHT, RACS,
Gateway or Veritas.
