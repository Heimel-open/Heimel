# Seed: Compute abundance, infrastructure scarcity

**Seed version:** `infrastructure-scarcity/0.1`  
**Status:** infrastructure-economic hypothesis  
**Full report:** [Compute Abundance, Infrastructure Scarcity](../reports/42-compute-abundance-infrastructure-scarcity.md)

## Proposition

When compute capability and access expand, the binding constraint on AI
deployment may move to physical and financial infrastructure: power,
interconnection, cooling, construction capacity, equipment refresh and
financing.

## Adoption test

For a proposed deployment, measure both digital capacity and physical delivery
constraints. Test whether additional model capability changes the outcome, or
whether the limiting factor is instead megawatts, time-to-connect, cooling,
capital or other infrastructure capacity.

## Falsifiers and boundaries

Narrow the seed when observed deployments show digital capability or model
economics remain the binding constraint under comparable conditions. This is a
planning hypothesis, not investment advice, a forecast of every market or an
authorization mechanism.
