# Seed: Capability as commodity, outcome as moat

**Seed version:** `capability-commodity/0.1`  
**Status:** strategic hypothesis  
**Full report:** [AI Capability Is Becoming Commodity](../reports/44-ai-capability-is-becoming-commodity.md)

## Proposition

As general AI capability becomes more replaceable, durable advantage shifts
from access to a particular model toward governed outcomes: trusted context,
domain integration, evidence, distribution and the ability to complete work
reliably under constraints.

## Adoption test

Replace the model or execution carrier while holding the outcome contract,
acceptance criteria and evidence requirements constant. Measure whether value,
trust and completion quality persist because of the surrounding system rather
than the identity of the model.

## Falsifiers and boundaries

The hypothesis weakens where model-specific capability remains the durable
source of value after credible substitution tests. It is not a claim that
frontier models are interchangeable today, nor a market guarantee or an
authorization rule.
