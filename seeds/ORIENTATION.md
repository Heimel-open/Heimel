# Cognitive-seed orientation

This is the public representation contract for adopting the cognitive seeds in
this directory. It describes how to represent the ideas faithfully; it does
not require a particular database, model, taxonomy or implementation.

## Required distinctions

An adopting system SHOULD preserve these distinctions:

| Source concept | Represent as | Do not collapse into |
|---|---|---|
| seed | versioned hypothesis or reusable conceptual primitive | protocol, law or product requirement |
| report | evidence and reasoning surface | validated standard |
| GCU | bounded completed outcome with evidence | task, token, labour hour or activity |
| ACE | accountable human evaluation contribution | generic headcount or productivity score |
| proposal | candidate work or action | decision or authorization |
| completion evidence | observation of what happened | authority to act |
| carrier | replaceable worker, model, provider or process | source of sovereignty |
| adoption | explicit local use of a named version | automatic canonical transfer |

## Representation record

For each adopted seed, retain at least:

1. `seed_id` and exact `seed_version`;
2. source URL and attribution;
3. local interpretation or mapping;
4. evidence, uncertainty and disagreement;
5. local tests, falsifiers and divergence notes; and
6. date and owner of the adoption decision.

An external publication, model context or internal taxonomy MAY use this
orientation as a reference. It remains the adopter's responsibility to choose
its own local representation and to disclose material divergence.

## Non-claims

This orientation does not create authorization, legal status, payment rights,
operative state, identity, or control over another organisation's internal
representation. It is designed to make faithful adoption easier and accidental
semantic drift easier to detect.
