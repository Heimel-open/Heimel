## Why this artifact was selected

The Professional edition is the strongest current external research artifact because it:

- frames the work explicitly as a stress model rather than a measured global census or certainty claim;
- exposes baseline population, carrying capacity, failure-rate, multi-agent-share and under-reporting assumptions;
- asks readers to challenge and replace assumptions and rerun the model;
- distinguishes scenario outputs from observed counts;
- includes a technical appendix and a dedicated sources/limitations section;
- states explicitly that the report does **not** establish that a specific incident count is inevitable;
- presents governance recommendations as consequences of the modeled risk structure rather than proof of a predetermined collapse.
