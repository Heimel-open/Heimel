# The Heimel universe

Heimel is one public and private universe with a category-based public
representation. Visitors should encounter ideas, evidence, maturity and
contracts—not a collection of historical project names.

## Public categories

| Category | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Orientation | How to read and adopt Heimel material |
| Cognitive seeds | Small, versioned ideas for independent adoption |
| Principles | Reusable design and measurement principles |
| Theses and hypotheses | Claims under investigation, with falsifiers |
| Claims and evidence | Bounded assertions and their evidence status |
| Research | Source-grounded investigations and syntheses |
| Reports and books | Longer-form published work |
| Protocols and standards | Normative interoperability and contract surfaces |
| Reference implementations | Small, reproducible implementations |
| Experiments | Reproduction, benchmarks and falsification |
| Discussions | Human interpretation, critique and adoption dialogue |
| Status | Maturity, provenance and current boundaries |

## Naming boundary

Historical working names and repository names may remain in provenance,
migration records and archival links when necessary to explain origin. They are
not public top-level identities or navigation categories. New public material
must be written and indexed as Heimel material.

Private sources remain outside Heimel. They must not appear in this universe,
including as history, provenance, an example or a migration source.

## Publication path

Research and books are first recorded as source-grounded material with author,
source, date, claim status and licence/IP review. Only a bounded synthesis that
can orient independent readers and state its uncertainty may become a cognitive
seed. A seed does not become a protocol, runtime, product or authority source by
being published here.
