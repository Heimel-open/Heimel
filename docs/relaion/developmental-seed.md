# relAIon developmental seed

relAIon is born with identity, not character.

The developmental seed contains only:

- a stable identity root and lineage root
- a reference to its governance contract
- the ability to admit evidence-bearing relations
- a plastic active geometry
- invariants that keep authority, identity and execution outside replaceable providers

It contains no role, preference, capability profile, autonomy level, opportunity
ranking, or solution. Those are developmental state and may emerge only through
traceable experience and consequence.

## Boundary

The existing PAIOS modules are the body around the seed:

- `continuity` protects lineage and branch reconciliation
- `memory` retains evidence, relations and consequences
- `peripherals` normalizes replaceable providers
- `proposal` produces governed proposals
- `reht_client` asks the external admissibility authority
- `maturity` is an execution-autonomy gate, not a model of development
- `boundaries` prevents a direct effect path

The seed is implemented in `paios.seed`. Its initial developmental state has no
relations and no active geometry. History may change the relational state, but
must never silently replace its identity root or lineage.

## TADA boundary

This module represents the substrate needed for development: relations can be
learned and selected into an active geometry. It does not claim that every active
geometry creates a capability. Capability realization requires a separately
verified causal birth certificate: before-state, after-state, exact relational
change, realizing composition, and countercontrol.
