# Active Inference, Free Energy Principle, and Framleis

## Summary

This note captures conceptual links between Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle / Active Inference and the Framleis / τ framework.

### Main correspondences

- Process before identity:
  - Active Inference treats cognition as continuous inference.
  - Framleis formalizes process F as ontologically prior to identity (fixed point).

- Prediction error vs τ:
  - Prediction error measures mismatch between model and observations.
  - τ may represent structural coherence of internal representations.
  - Hypothesis: systems minimize prediction error while maintaining τ within a stable regime.

- Curiosity:
  - Active Inference values epistemic information alongside reward.
  - α can be interpreted as balancing exploration and exploitation.

- Goldilocks:
  - Both frameworks imply an optimal operating region between excessive certainty and excessive uncertainty.

- Continuous loop:
  - Perception and action form one inference cycle.
  - Framleis describes identity as emerging through continuous iteration.

## Research direction

Do not claim that Φ-law or Framleis is the Free Energy Principle.

Instead investigate whether τ is an observable macro-variable describing structural coherence inside Active Inference systems.
