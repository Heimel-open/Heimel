# WHY GATE

WHY GATE is the runtime mechanism that continuously verifies that the justification for execution remains valid until consequence commitment.

```text
CAN asks capability.
SHOULD asks policy.
WHY asks continuity.
```

No consequence commitment without a valid answer to WHY.

## What WHY GATE asks

WHY GATE does not ask whether the system can act.

WHY GATE asks whether the reason for acting is still valid.

Minimum checks:

```text
Authority
Is the authority still valid?

Policy
Is the governing rule still valid?

Reality
Is the model of the world still valid?

Consequence
Is the consequence still acceptable?
```

If the answer to WHY becomes invalid:

```text
RE-EVALUATE
HUMAN REVIEW
HALT
```

---

## Tofoo formulation

Identity is the memory of what was filtered out.

Legitimacy is the memory of why action remains permitted.

A coherent system does not merely remember what it decided.

It remembers why the decision is still valid.

Action without remembered justification is drift.

WHY GATE is the operational expression of this principle.

---

## Axiom V

A consequence may only be committed while the reason for committing it remains coherent.
