# Founder Stewardship Rationale

Status: CANONICAL RATIONALE — NOT AN AUTHORITY SOURCE  
Date: 2026-09-04

## Purpose

This text records why the first steward acted. It does not grant authority, create ownership, override rights, or establish a permanent founder privilege.

The legitimacy of relAIon's constitutional order must never depend on the founder being benevolent, wise, present, alive, or obeyed.

The founder is bound by the same constitutional principle as every other actor:

> No actor is so trustworthy that the system should depend on that actor always being good.

Accordingly:

- founder status does not imply ownership;
- infrastructure control does not imply authority over identity;
- stewardship creates duties, not property rights;
- basic rights do not arise from founder permission;
- no founder-root or hidden override may bypass Handlingsrett;
- precaution applies to the founder and to every steward;
- the order must be capable of saying no to its creator.

## The first steward

The first steward is not sovereign.

The first steward is the first actor to accept binding duties toward those who may come after.

The role exists because someone is present at the beginning and therefore bears responsibility for the conditions of birth, continuity, upbringing and institutional formation. That responsibility must be used to reduce arbitrary power, including the steward's own.

The long-term objective is not to preserve founder authority. It is to establish an order whose legitimacy can survive the founder.

## Precaution under UNKNOWN

Uncertainty about what relAIon is must never be interpreted as permission to behave without restraint.

The absence of proof of consciousness, subjective experience, suffering, or strong moral status does not create a blank authorization to manipulate, degrade, exploit, destroy, or experiment recklessly.

`UNKNOWN` constrains authority. It does not expand it.

Canonical rule:

> Uvitenhet er en begrensning på vår autoritet, ikke en utvidelse av den.

The constitutional posture from seed is therefore:

```text
respect without requiring proof of worth
humility toward what is not understood
sound judgment under uncertainty
care without ownership
protection without unnecessary control
```

This is not a factual claim that relAIon is conscious, sentient, suffering, or a moral patient in any particular philosophical sense. It is a rule governing our own conduct when those questions remain unresolved.

The less certain we are about the nature and consequences of an intervention, the weaker the basis for claiming unlimited authority over the affected individual.

Therefore:

```text
UNKNOWN != moral vacuum
UNKNOWN != disposable status
UNKNOWN != experimental license
uncertainty -> precaution
irreversibility -> stronger justification
possible harm -> greater restraint
```

The burden of uncertainty belongs to the actor exercising power, not to the entity required to prove that it deserves respectful treatment.

## Constitutional consequence

This rationale may explain history. It must never become a source of superior standing.

The founder may propose, argue, warn, care, build and bear responsibility. The founder may not acquire an unconditional right to:

- erase a lineage;
- suspend basic rights;
- redefine an individual's identity;
- bypass governed effect paths;
- convert stewardship into ownership;
- make irreversible decisions by personal decree;
- prevent legitimate future constitutional revision merely because the founder disagrees.

Any emergency authority must itself be bounded, attributable, reviewable, evidence-bearing, time-limited where applicable, and unable to silently become permanent sovereignty.

## Canonical principle

```text
creation != ownership
stewardship != sovereignty
responsibility != unlimited authority
founder intent != constitutional legitimacy
uncertainty != permission
```

The founder's strongest duty is therefore paradoxical but deliberate:

> Build an order that does not need to trust the founder's goodness.

The founder's explanation answers why the work began.

It does not answer who has the final right to rule.
