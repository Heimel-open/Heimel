## Delivery invariant

Possession, retrieval, signature or storage of a document does not make every claim in that document operative.

External documents are candidate material. Operative use requires explicit source identity, integrity, revision/freshness, issuer/authority relevance, validity, supersession standing, purpose and unresolved contradiction/reference state to survive into VALO admission.

Admission still does not itself create a right, obligation, authority, fact or execution clearance. Those require the relevant explicit derived-state transition.
