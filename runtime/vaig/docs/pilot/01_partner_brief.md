# Partner Brief — VAIG Pilot

Status: working brief  
Audience: Svein / Frank / strategic pilot discussion  
Disclosure level: controlled, non-repo, non-technical-detail first meeting

## 1. Opening position

VAIG is not a chatbot and not a model.

VAIG is a runtime governance layer for the point where AI output becomes action.

The current repository contains a testable governance runtime, pre-intent evidence gate, refusal lifecycle, receipt chain and ACS packet schema.

The next step is not more theory.

The next step is one bounded pilot and the correct company structure.

## 2. Problem

Enterprise AI is moving from recommendation into action.

When that happens, the key risk is not only that the model gives a wrong answer.

The key risk is that the AI changes what can happen next inside a workflow.

Current governance often appears after the fact: policy, review, audit, legal reconstruction.

VAIG moves control before consequence.

## 3. What exists now

Current technical core includes:

- VAIG runtime governance stack
- EvidenceCondition pre-intent gate
- RiskContract evidence and policy binding
- IntentFactory enforcement
- AARM decision structure
- RRP refusal lifecycle
- accountability thread
- receipt / WORM audit direction
- ACS / VACS packet and receipt schema
- EU Article 6 / execution-path framing

## 4. What this means commercially

This is not just AI safety language.

It is infrastructure for governed AI execution.

Potential value areas:

- GRC
- audit evidence
- AI risk management
- regulated enterprise workflows
- insurance evidence
- energy and infrastructure governance
- internal control for agentic AI

## 5. Pilot ask

We need one credible workflow.

A good first pilot has:

- one AI-assisted task
- one possible consequential action
- one evidence requirement
- one policy boundary
- one human authority point
- one refusal case
- one receipt report

Candidate pilot areas:

1. AI-assisted contract / supplier review.
2. AI-assisted risk or incident summary.
3. AI agent preparing an operational recommendation.
4. AI-assisted GRC or compliance workflow.
5. Energy / infrastructure risk report where AI output can trigger escalation.

## 6. What partner gets from pilot

The pilot should produce:

- sample ACS packet
- EvidenceCondition record
- RiskContract snapshot
- AARM decision
- Refusal Object if blocked
- Authority Visibility / assignment output
- accountability thread
- receipt
- short governance report

## 7. What partner does not get initially

## 8. Company question

The strategic question is whether this should be built as an independent European AI governance infrastructure company.

Key questions for discussion:

- Who can open the first credible industrial pilot?
- Who can advise on GRC, insurance and energy relevance?
- What should be the first six-month capitalization?
- What role should Svein / Frank / Cegal have, if any?
- What must remain owned and protected before any pilot?

## 9. Suggested meeting language

I think the research phase is over enough to stop adding theory.

VAIG is not a finished enterprise product, but the core is testable.

The repo now contains runtime governance, pre-intent evidence gating, refusal lifecycle, receipt chain and a protocol seed.

The question is whether we structure this as an independent company and run one bounded industrial pilot.

## 10. Do not say

Do not say:

- this is fully production certified
- this guarantees EU AI Act compliance
- this guarantees insurance acceptance
- ACS / VACS is already a market standard
- the full repo should be opened immediately

## 11. One-line summary

VAIG controls the boundary where AI output becomes action and produces the evidence needed to audit what happened.
