## 2. LINKEDIN ALGORITHM MECHANICS (2025-2026)

### The 4-Stage Distribution Process

**Stage 1: Quality Check (first 60 minutes)**
- Spam/low-quality/high-quality classification
- Excessive hashtags (>5) = penalty
- Multiple posts <24h = reduced reach on newest
- Engagement bait ("like if you agree") = penalty
- External links = significant reach reduction

**Stage 2: Golden Window (first 2 hours)**
- Small sample audience test
- Key signals: dwell time, 1st-degree connection response, save rate, 15+ word comments
- If <500 impressions in first hour, post likely dies
- Mobile formatting matters enormously

**Stage 3: 8-Hour Review**
- Identity matching: content shown to similar professional backgrounds
- Content relevance: keyword/topic matching to interested users
- Relationship strength: regular engagers see your content FIRST

**Stage 4: Final Push (24 hours)**
- High performers distributed to 2nd/3rd degree connections
- Low performers stop being promoted
- Posting new content can cut off reach on previous post

### What This Means for Valo-Twin:

1. **Scheduling intelligence** matters — posting at optimal times for Njål's audience timezone
2. **The first hour is everything** — a draft that passes the VALO gate but is posted at the wrong time still fails
3. **Comments > likes** — the algorithm weights comment velocity (15+ words) far above reactions
4. **Relationship strength** — Valo-Twin's watchlist should track who engages most, prioritize those relationships
5. **Dwell time** — document carousels and text posts outperform link posts (keep users ON LinkedIn)

## 3. NETWORK BUILDING & AUTHORITY SYSTEMS

### 4 Growth Motions Working for B2B Founders in 2026

**Motion 1: Connection-led Growth**
- 100 targeted connection requests/week (not random, signal-based)
- Target: 30% acceptance rate = ~1,500 new relevant connections/year
- Flow: Social listening → Enrichment → Outreach
- Key: Signal over scale. Research before requesting.

**Motion 2: Posting-led Growth**
- 2-5 posts/week with clear POV
- 65% authority (teach) / 25% personal (story) / 10% soft sales
- Content pillars defined, backlog constantly captured
- Batched from real business activity (calls, Slack, decks)

**Motion 3: Conversation-led Growth**
- 15-20 min/day commenting on posts where ICP hangs out
- Treat comments as mini-posts — educate 90% of the time
- Track 3+ engagement signals → move to DM
- Visibility through participation, not just publishing

**Motion 4: Partner/Community/Collab-led**
- Guest posts on domains cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity
- Podcast appearances (10-15 pitches/quarter)
- Expert source responses (HARO, Featured: 3-5/week)
- Community partnerships with complementary tools

### Profile Optimization Checklist
- Headline: niche + problem you solve (NOT "Founder at XYZ")
- About section: conversion-focused, not resume
- Featured section: strong CTA/offer (most leave empty)
- Creator Mode enabled (changes "Connect" to "Follow")
- SSI score above 70 = 45% higher connection acceptance
