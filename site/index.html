HEIMEL is the mechanically enforceable boundary between agent intent and real-world consequence.

Agents can plan freely.
Consequences need authority.

HEIMEL sits underneath agent frameworks, tools and orchestration layers. It resolves authority again when an action is about to become consequential, binds that authority to the exact effect, and records what actually happened.

The boundary is the product.

01

Fresh authority

Previous approval is not enough. Authority is checked again at consequence time.

02

Exact binding

The permit is bound to the specific state, action and effect that was authorized.

03

One governed path

No direct effect path. Consequential actions pass through the boundary or they do not happen.

04

One-shot permits

Execution authority is not a reusable bearer token. Replay is rejected.

05

Verifiable evidence

The system records what was authorized, what executed and which consequence occurred.

06

Unknown fails closed

Missing authority, mappings or bindings do not silently degrade into permission.
