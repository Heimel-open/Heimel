The governed path

Freedom to think.
Authority to act.

Models and agents can reason, plan and propose. Heimel intervenes only where intent becomes consequence—resolving current authority for the exact action, against the exact state, before execution.

Designed for trust

The boundary is the product.

Heimel is not another agent platform. It is a narrow, independently testable infrastructure layer for any system capable of creating real effects.

01

Fresh authority

Past approval cannot silently survive revocation, expiry or changed conditions.

02

Exact binding

A permit applies only to the state, action and effect that were authorized.

03

Fail closed

Missing authority, mappings or evidence never degrade into permission.

04

One-shot permits

Execution authority is not a reusable bearer token. Replay is rejected.

05

No direct effect path

Consequential actions pass through the boundary or they do not happen.

06

Verifiable evidence

The system retains the operative decision, execution and observed effect.
