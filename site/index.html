From shadow to scale

Change when you know.
Not when you guess.

Start with no effect authority. Let the SI make the decisions it would have made. Measure those decisions against real outcomes. When performance is good enough, expand its scope deliberately. Heimel keeps that progression observable, bounded and reversible.

How trust is kept honest

Trust is earned in operation.

The goal is scalable SI. Authority is one of the mechanisms that makes that possible. Heimel keeps every expansion of autonomy tied to current limits, observed behavior and verifiable evidence.

01

Shadow first

Learn what the SI would do before giving it consequential control.

02

Evidence over confidence

Trust grows from observed performance, not model claims or demos.

03

Bounded expansion

Responsibility grows by proven scope, not by blanket permission.

04

Fresh authority

The SI can act only inside authority that is valid at the moment of consequence.

05

No hidden bypass

Consequential actions stay on the governed path as autonomy increases.

06

Replayable evidence

You can reconstruct what it knew, what it chose and what actually happened.

The point is not to keep SI constrained forever. The point is to know when it has earned the right to do more.
