Capability is not authority. Consequences require authority.

Keep your identity provider, policy engine, agent framework and workflow stack. Heimel sits below them, resolves fresh authority for the exact proposed effect, and releases execution only through the governed consequence path.

Designed for trust

The boundary is the product.

Not AI governance. Not IAM. Not a generic policy engine. A narrow, independently testable authority layer at the point where intention can become consequence.

Fresh authority

Past approval cannot silently survive revocation, expiry or changed conditions.

Delegation attenuates

Delegated authority cannot exceed its origin, scope, purpose, expiry or constraints.

Exact binding

A permit applies only to the state, action and effect that were authorized.

Consequence capability after the gate

Downstream credentials and effect-bearing capability belong behind the governed boundary wherever possible.

No direct effect path

Consequential actions pass through the boundary or they do not happen.

Evidence before allow

An ALLOW is attributable before execution authority is released; actual effect is proven separately.
